How to Order Custom Corrugated Slip Sheets: Sizes, Caliper, and Lead Times

Corrugatedslipsheet Editorial • September 2026

Why Slip Sheet Specifications Matter

Corrugated slip sheets — flat sheets of twin-wall PP corrugated placed between pallet layers or used in push-pull pallet handling — are often treated as a commodity. In practice, the right specification makes a significant difference in handling performance, load integrity, and cost per use. The wrong caliper collapses under load. The wrong size creates overhang that interferes with push-pull equipment. Edges left untreated fray and catch on conveyor rollers.

This guide walks procurement managers and logistics operations buyers through the steps to specify and order custom corrugated slip sheets that perform reliably from day one.

Step 1: Determine the Required Footprint

Slip sheet footprint is defined by the pallet size it's intended to serve and the application type:

  • Full pallet slip sheets: Sized to match pallet footprint, typically 48" x 40" (GMA standard) or 1200mm x 1000mm (Euro). Add 1–2" overhang on all sides if using push-pull equipment, as the tab must extend beyond the pallet edge for the gripper to engage.
  • Tier sheets / layer pads: Sized to the interior of the box or container layer they separate. Dimensions should allow the sheet to lie flat without buckling or bridging at corners.
  • Custom footprints: Operations with non-standard pallets, floor-stacked loads, or specific racking configurations may require dimensions outside standard sizes.

Provide both length and width dimensions in millimeters or inches. If flute direction matters for your application — some push-pull systems prefer flutes running parallel to the pull direction for maximum rigidity under the gripper — specify this in your order.

Step 2: Select Wall Caliper

Slip sheet caliper is selected based on load weight, stacking height, and whether the sheet is a full-pallet base or an inter-layer pad:

  • 2mm: Layer pads and tier sheets for light products; not suitable for push-pull base sheets
  • 3mm: Light-duty tier sheets; push-pull base sheets for loads under 600 kg
  • 4mm: Standard push-pull slip sheets for moderate loads; common for consumer goods distribution
  • 5mm or 6mm: Heavy-duty push-pull or high-stacking applications; food production and beverage pallets

For push-pull applications, verify that the slip sheet supplier provides compression test data at your expected load weight. A 4mm PP corrugated sheet rated for 800 kg static load may still deflect under dynamic push-pull handling — ask for dynamic load performance data as well.

Step 3: Specify Edge Treatment

Exposed cut edges on PP corrugated slip sheets can fray, snag conveyor components, or present a hygiene concern in food environments. Edge treatment options include:

  • Hot-knife cut edges: Standard finish; clean but unsealed
  • Heat-sealed edges: Melted and sealed to prevent fraying; adds cost but extends sheet life in high-cycle applications
  • Folded and taped edges: Additional layer of material folded over the cut edge; used for push-pull tabs where edge rigidity is critical

For food and pharmaceutical environments, sealed edges are preferred because open flute channels in unsealed cut edges can harbor moisture and debris.

Step 4: Color and Identification

PP corrugated slip sheets are available in the same color range as other corrugated plastic products — black, white, grey, blue, green, yellow, red. Color coding slip sheets by product zone, temperature zone, or load type is a simple and effective warehouse management tool. Screen-printed text, part numbers, or barcodes can be added for identification in high-SKU environments.

Step 5: Confirm MOQ and Lead Times

Standard sizes in common calipers may be available from stock at distributors, but custom sizes and colors require production runs with associated minimum order quantities. Typical MOQs for custom slip sheets from overseas manufacturers:

  • Standard size, single color: 500–1,000 sheets
  • Custom size or color: 1,000–2,500 sheets
  • With print: 2,000+ sheets due to setup costs

Step 6: Request Samples Before Full Order

For new custom specifications, always request a pre-production sample or first-article inspection sample before approving a full production run. Verify dimensions, caliper (measured with calipers, not estimated), color accuracy, edge treatment quality, and load performance before committing to a full order quantity. Most manufacturers can produce a small sample quantity within the standard lead time for a nominal fee.

Slip Sheet Order Specification Checklist

  • Sheet dimensions (L x W in mm or inches)
  • Wall caliper (mm)
  • Flute orientation (if directional performance is required)
  • Edge treatment (cut, heat-sealed, folded)
  • Color
  • Print requirements (if any)
  • FDA food-contact compliance required (yes/no)
  • Quantity per order and annual volume estimate
  • Delivery port or warehouse destination
  • Sample requirement before full production approval
