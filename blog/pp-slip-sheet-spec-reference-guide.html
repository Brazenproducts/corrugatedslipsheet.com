PP Corrugated Slip Sheet Spec Reference: Thickness, Sizes, and Load Capacity

Corrugatedslipsheet Editorial • September 2026

Specifying PP Corrugated Slip Sheets: A Precision Exercise

Corrugated plastic slip sheets are a precision product. Unlike general packaging, slip sheet performance is highly sensitive to dimensional tolerances, surface friction characteristics, and edge strength — small deviations from specification can mean the difference between a clean pallet transfer and a product spill. This reference guide is for procurement managers and materials handling engineers specifying PP corrugated slip sheets for push-pull, forklift platen, or tier sheet applications.

Material Construction and Performance Properties

PP corrugated slip sheets use twin-wall fluted polypropylene construction — the same base material as corrugated plastic boxes — but are specified and tested differently. For slip sheet applications, the primary performance metrics are:

  • Flexural stiffness: The sheet must deflect minimally under load during push-pull transfer. Insufficient stiffness causes sheet folding and load instability.
  • Edge strength: The leading edge — gripped by the forklift platen or push-pull head — must resist buckling and tearing across the full load width during transfer cycles.
  • Surface friction: The product-contact surface (typically the top) should have appropriate friction for your handling equipment and product type. Some applications require low-friction surfaces; others require moderate grip for load stability.
  • Weight: Sufficient mass to remain flat and stable during loading and transit without adding unnecessary freight weight.

Standard Thickness Options and Selection Guide

PP corrugated slip sheets are typically available in 2mm, 3mm, 4mm, and 5mm wall thicknesses. Selection guidance by application:

  • 2mm: Light-duty tier sheets between pallet layers. Low load capacity; used for layer separation rather than full pallet push-pull handling.
  • 3mm: Standard slip sheet for loads up to approximately 500kg on 1000×1200mm footprint. Suitable for most consumer packaged goods and produce applications.
  • 4mm: Medium-to-heavy duty pallet slip sheets for loads of 500–1,000kg. Recommended for industrial goods, hardware, and heavy produce applications.
  • 5mm: Heavy-duty applications exceeding 1,000kg pallet load. Appropriate for steel components, machinery parts, or high-stack pallets in industrial facilities.

Standard and Custom Footprint Sizes

Slip sheet footprint should match or slightly exceed the pallet footprint to ensure full load support and clean transfer. Common standard sizes:

  • 1200×1000mm — GMA/ISO pallet compatible
  • 1200×800mm — Euro pallet compatible
  • 48"×40" / 1219×1016mm — US GMA pallet standard
  • 1100×1100mm — Asia pallet standard

For operations with non-standard pallet dimensions or tab extensions required for push-pull equipment, custom sizes are the correct specification. Custom corrugated plastic sheets are manufactured to exact footprint and tab dimension requirements with no tooling surcharge for sheet-format products.

Tab Configuration for Push-Pull Equipment

Push-pull slip sheet attachments grip a protruding tab on the leading edge of the slip sheet to pull the pallet load onto the forklift platen. Tab specifications are critical and must match the push-pull head design:

  • Tab width: Full-width tabs are standard; partial-width tabs (e.g., 600mm centered) are available for narrower push-pull heads
  • Tab depth: Typically 50–75mm protrusion beyond the pallet footprint; confirm with your equipment supplier before finalizing
  • Tab reinforcement: Some push-pull systems require double-ply tab reinforcement to prevent tearing at high load weights
  • Tab position: Single-tab (one side) for standard push-pull; four-tab (all sides) for multi-directional handling or automated systems

Load Capacity Reference Data

Slip sheet load capacity depends on wall thickness, flute orientation relative to load direction, and pallet footprint. General reference benchmarks for 1200×1000mm footprint:

  • 3mm PP corrugated: 400–600kg dynamic load (push-pull operation)
  • 4mm PP corrugated: 700–1,000kg dynamic load
  • 5mm PP corrugated: 1,000–1,500kg dynamic load

Request static and dynamic load test data from your supplier before finalizing specification. ASTM D1185 provides a standard test method for pallets and related structures, including slip sheets, and can serve as the basis for acceptance testing.

Reuse Cycles and End-of-Life

PP corrugated slip sheets are typically specified for multiple-use applications, with cycle life depending on handling conditions. In controlled warehouse environments, 10–30 cycles is achievable. In rougher handling or outdoor staging environments, cycle counts are lower. At end of useful life, PP corrugated slip sheets are recyclable through #5 polypropylene recovery streams in most North American markets.
