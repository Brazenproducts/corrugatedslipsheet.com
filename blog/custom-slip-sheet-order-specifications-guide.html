Custom Slip Sheet Orders: Specifications, Lead Times, and Sourcing from Asia

Corrugatedslipsheet Editorial • September 2026

Why Custom Slip Sheets Require Different Sourcing Than Standard Pallets

Standard 48" × 40" slip sheets are commodity products available from multiple domestic and import distributors. However, procurement teams managing specialized applications—pharmaceutical distribution, food-grade handling, export containers, or unusual pallet configurations—frequently require custom dimensions, proprietary material grades, or specific performance certifications that stock products cannot provide. Custom slip sheet orders involve longer lead times, tooling investments, and quality validation steps that differ fundamentally from standard purchasing workflows.

This guide covers the complete process for sourcing custom PP corrugated slip sheets from Asian manufacturers, with particular focus on food and pharmaceutical compliance, specification development, and total cost optimization.

Defining Specifications: What Goes Into a Custom Slip Sheet Order

A complete slip sheet specification includes dimensions, material grade, performance requirements, and certifications. Vague or incomplete specs are the primary cause of quality issues and delivery delays in custom orders. Every custom order should specify:

  • Dimensions: Length, width, and thickness in metric units (mm) with tolerance ranges (+/- 2mm is standard)
  • Material: Virgin PP homopolymer or copolymer, FDA-compliant resin, and any specialty additives (UV stabilizer, anti-static, flame retardant)
  • Gauge: 3mm for lightweight applications; 4-5mm for standard fork-truck handling; 6mm for heavy loads or abusive environments
  • Edge treatment: Plain (cut edges), heat-sealed, or capped with extruded PP edge trim
  • Surface treatment: Anti-slip coating (mandatory for palletized loads), smooth finish, or perforated for drainage
  • Color: Natural (translucent white), custom color match, or black for UV resistance
  • Certification requirements: FDA food contact, USDA suitability, ISO 9001 manufacturing quality, ISTA testing protocols

PP corrugated slip sheets from Elipacko are available in standard 48" × 40" and 42" × 42" sizes with custom dimensions from 24" × 24" up to 60" × 96". Material grade defaults to FDA-compliant virgin PP homopolymer unless otherwise specified.

Tooling and Setup Costs: Understanding the Investment

Corrugated plastic slip sheets are extruded as flat sheets and then cut to size. Standard sizes use existing cutting dies with no tooling charge. Custom dimensions require new cutting dies, which represent the primary non-recurring engineering cost in custom orders.

Die costs for custom slip sheet sizes:

  • Straight-cut rectangular dies: $800-$1,500. Suitable for most custom sizes; simple geometry only.
  • Contour-cut dies (rounded corners, notches, tapered profiles): $2,000-$4,500. Required when slip sheets need features beyond simple rectangles.
  • Multi-cavity dies: $4,000-$8,000. Allow simultaneous cutting of multiple sizes or complex shapes. Economical for high-volume orders (50,000+ units annually).

For orders under 10,000 units, tooling costs typically add $0.15-$0.40 per unit. At volumes above 20,000 units annually, tooling can be amortized below $0.10 per unit, making custom sizing cost-competitive with standard sizes.

Minimum Order Quantities and Volume Commitments

Asian manufacturers generally require minimum order quantities (MOQs) of 1,000-5,000 units per size for custom dimensions. Stock sizes (48" × 40", 42" × 42") typically have no MOQ or MOQs of 200-500 units. Understanding MOQ structure is critical for budget planning, especially when qualifying multiple custom sizes simultaneously.

Volume discount tiers for custom PP slip sheets (4mm gauge, standard edge treatment):

  • 1,000-4,999 units: $4.80-$5.20 per unit
  • 5,000-9,999 units: $4.20-$4.60 per unit
  • 10,000-24,999 units: $3.80-$4.20 per unit
  • 25,000+ units: $3.20-$3.60 per unit (requires annual commitment)

Lead times scale inversely with order size. A 1,000-unit custom order may ship in 6-8 weeks; a 20,000-unit order requires 10-14 weeks due to extrusion line scheduling. Air freight can reduce lead time to 3-4 weeks but adds $0.80-$1.20 per unit to landed cost.

Antidumping Duty and Trade Compliance

Polypropylene corrugated products from China currently face antidumping (AD) duties of 68-70%, making Thai and Vietnamese production significantly more cost-competitive for US importers. Elipacko manufactures PP corrugated slip sheets in Thailand and Vietnam—both countries with 0% ADD duties on PP corrugate—providing a critical cost advantage over Chinese-origin alternatives.

For FDA food-contact applications, documentation requirements include:

  • FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 compliance letter from resin supplier
  • Food-contact notification (FCN) if using new resin additives
  • Certificate of Analysis (COA) per production lot
  • Bill of materials declaring all substrate layers and adhesives

US Customs requires country of origin marking on each bale or packaging unit. For transshipment concerns (goods routed through a third country to avoid tariffs), maintain documentation showing direct shipment from manufacturing country with original Certificate of Origin.

Quality Validation: Receiving Inspection for Custom Slip Sheets

Custom orders warrant more rigorous receiving inspection than standard products because tooling changes or material substitutions can occur between orders. Establish incoming quality specifications before placing orders:

  • Dimensions: Measure length, width, and thickness at 4 corners and center. Reject if any measurement exceeds tolerance.
  • Flute orientation: Verify flute direction matches specification (critical for load capacity).
  • Surface defects: Visual inspection for scratches, delamination, contamination, or color variation.
  • Edge quality: Heat-sealed edges should be fully fused with no gaps or fraying.
  • Compression test: Sample 3-5 units per 1,000 and test to documented load rating.

Elipacko provides pre-shipment inspection reports and can arrange third-party inspection (SGS, Bureau Veritas) for orders exceeding $10,000 FOB value. Inspection cost runs $300-$600 per order plus inspector travel for on-site visits.

Payment Terms and Risk Management

Standard payment terms for custom slip sheet orders from Asia are 30% deposit at order placement, 70% against shipping documents (BL or AWB). Letters of Credit (LC) are available for orders exceeding $25,000 and provide the highest level of payment security for first-time transactions with new suppliers.

Key contractual protections to include:

  • Specific dimensional and performance tolerances (not "industry standard")
  • Remedy clause for non-conforming material (rejection, re-work, or price reduction)
  • Inspection period (10 business days after receipt to file claims)
  • Warranty period (minimum 90 days for manufacturing defects)
  • Force majeure language covering port closures, raw material shortages, and natural disasters

Custom Corrugated Plastic Sheets Beyond Slip Sheets

While slip sheets represent a high-volume application, custom PP corrugated sheet can serve broader packaging needs: layer pads, pallet collars, void fill panels, protective corner guards, and agricultural trays. Custom corrugated plastic sheets from Elipacko support widths up to 96" and lengths limited only by logistics (standard truck trailer is 53').

For procurement teams standardizing on PP corrugated across multiple applications, consolidated orders (slip sheets + other sheet products in same material gauge) reduce per-unit tooling costs and simplify supplier management. Quarterly ordering cadence with 60-90 day supply coverage balances carrying cost against price discounts for larger shipments.
